# Terms of Service — BigClipz

_Last updated: 30/08/2026_

BigClipz identifies potentially interesting moments from live streams,
prepares short clips with a suggested caption, and — only after a human
reviewer approves a clip — publishes it to the BigClipz TikTok account.

## Content rights
BigClipz publishes clips under a fair use basis. If you are a streamer
and believe your content was posted without appropriate basis, contact
us at BigClipz@protonmail.com and we will review the request promptly.

## No unauthorized posting
BigClipz will never publish a clip without human review and approval.
