# Privacy Policy — BigClipz

_Last updated: 30/08/2026_

BigClipz identifies and publishes highlight
clips from live streams to the BigClipz TikTok account. This policy
explains what data we collect and how it's used.

## What we collect
- **TikTok account data**: BigClipz's own TikTok account access token
  and profile info, used to publish approved clips.
- **Stream content**: Video clips and transcripts sourced from public
  live streams, used to identify and prepare highlight-worthy moments.
- **Usage data**: Basic logs (timestamps, which clips were approved/
  rejected) to operate and troubleshoot the service.

## How we use it
- To publish approved clips to the BigClipz TikTok account.
- To operate, maintain, and improve BigClipz.
- We do not sell data or share it with third parties except as required
  to provide the service (e.g., TikTok's own API) or by law.

## Content sourcing
BigClipz clips content from live streams for republishing under a fair
use basis. Streamers who believe a clip was posted without appropriate
basis, or who want a clip removed, can contact us at
BigClipz@protonmail.com and we will review the request promptly.

## Data retention
We retain clip and transcript data for 1 week, then delete it.

## Your rights (for streamers featured in clips)
If you are a streamer and want a clip removed or want to opt out of
being featured, contact us at BigClipz@protonmail.com.
